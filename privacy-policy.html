ScrollRank — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 July 2026

ScrollRank is a satirical screen-time app that counts how many Instagram Reels you watch and ranks you against friends. It is developed and operated by Muhammad Ehsan, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described below. This policy explains exactly what data the app touches, what leaves your phone, and what never does.

What we collect and why

  • Reel counts. With your explicit consent, ScrollRank uses Android's Accessibility service to detect when Instagram's Reels viewer is on your screen and counts Reels watched for 3 seconds or more. The raw activity — which Reels, when, for how long — stays on your device. Only your aggregate daily count and total watch seconds are uploaded, to place you on the leaderboard.
  • Account data. If you sign in with Google we receive your email address and a unique account ID. If you continue as a guest, an anonymous account ID is created with no personal details.
  • Profile you choose. A nickname, an optional Instagram username, and mascot cosmetics. Your username, mascot, and weekly Reel count are visible to other users on the leaderboard — that's the point of the app. Choose a username you're comfortable showing publicly.

What we do NOT collect

  • No message contents, passwords, or any on-screen content from Instagram or any other app. The accessibility service only reacts to the Reels screen appearing/disappearing.
  • No contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or browsing history.
  • No data from other apps on your device.

Where data lives

Server data (account, daily counts, friendships) is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and server functions on our behalf. All writes go through validated server functions; other users can only ever see your public leaderboard profile.

Third parties

We use two service providers, and only for the purposes described here:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and server functions.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google authenticates you and provides us your email address and account ID. Guest mode does not involve Google.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising or profiling. There are no ads, analytics SDKs, or trackers in ScrollRank.

Security

All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Data stored on our servers is protected by access rules that prevent any user from reading another user's data beyond the public leaderboard profile described above.

How long we keep data

Your account, scores, and friendships are kept for as long as your account exists. Leaderboard results are kept as part of your weekly history until your account is deleted. When you delete your account, all of it is removed from our servers immediately — see below. Deleting the app without deleting your account does not remove your server data; use the in-app delete option if that is what you want.

Your controls

  • Turn tracking off anytime in your phone's Accessibility settings.
  • Sign out in Settings → Account.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, scores, friendships, and leaderboard history from our servers, immediately.

Children

ScrollRank is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.
